## project_vmf_distribution.png

- Original statistical illustration for this portfolio, generated using SciPy's `vonmises_fisher.rvs` and Matplotlib.
- Reference: https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.vonmises_fisher.html
- 450 unit-vector samples per distribution, concentrations 2, 25 and 100, random seed 20261016.
- Mean directions are the normalized vectors (-0.75, -0.5, 0.45), (0.2, -0.3, -0.94), and (0.7, 0.65, 0.3).
- This visual explains vMF directions and concentrations. It is not manuscript data, a fitted clustering result, or evidence of the proposed method's performance.
